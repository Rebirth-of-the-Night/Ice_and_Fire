Once the egg was inside the pond,
I filled the pond  around the
egg in order to cool it down.
After waiting for around a short
time, I could not see any
difference with the egg. I
thought of removing the egg and
looking for another method of

hatching the egg, but I
remembered that dragons are
large beings that require a
great amount of time to grow.
Deciding not to be hasty,
I let the egg sit in the lake
for three days.

My decision was very successful!
The egg formed ice around itself
The egg finally hatched! Yet
the young dragon remained wild.
It had no owner and showed no
loyalty to the one who tended
its egg. I withdrew to safety.
