Lightning dragonsteel can be
forged in a lightning forge,
just like the fire and ice
kinds. It can be made into
tools, armor, and dragon armor.










Keep in mind that a forge
can be built for lightning
dragons, using the sheer
power of their bolts.









Lightning dragonsteel strikes
its targets with lightning
bolts and deals high damage,
even to large dragons.
Dragonsteel armor also gives
more protection against
dragon breath attacks.
