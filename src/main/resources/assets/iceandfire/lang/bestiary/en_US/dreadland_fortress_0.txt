Rising from the frozen ruins of
the Dreadlands, the Dread
Fortress is the seat of the
Dread Queen and the heart of
her undead legions.

Its walls are not meant to be
broken. While the Queen lives,
almost every block of the
fortress resists mining and
building, apart from torches,
snow, chests, spawners and
cobblestone. A warning appears
whenever you try.

Only when she falls does the
castle's protection fade away.



Its garrison is vast. Dread
spawners keep the halls full
of undead, and castle ballistas
wake and open fire as soon as
a stranger comes near.
Near the Queen, spawners call
forth her dragon, Black Frost.
Bring dragonsteel, healing and
allies.
