    After harvesting dragon
blood by using a glass bottle
on a dragon's corpse, I finally
figured out how to use it
effectively as a weapon.









By combining fire, ice or
lightning blood with a dragon
bone sword, I was able to infuse
them into potent weapons against
the other dragon types.
opposite dragon.







Fire Infused swords ignite
their target into flames,
and do extra damage against
Ice Dragons. Ice Infused swords
slow down their target, and do
extra damage to Fire Dragons.
Lightning Infused swords strike
foes with bolts, hurting both.
Fire and Ice swords also knock back.


Dragon blood can also be used
to preserve dragons.
