Deep inside the Dread Fortress
waits the ruler of the undead,
the Dread Queen.




She is extremely tough, with
far more health than any of
her servants, and heavy armor
that blunts weaker weapons.

Crowned and clad in dark blue,
the undead queen swings the
Blade of the Dreaded Queen and
carries the Staff of the Dreaded
Queen in her other hand.







Until she falls, the fortress
walls resist all but a few
blocks, like torches, snow,
spawners and chests.

Slain, she drops:







Once she has fallen, the snow
of the Dreadlands slowly fades.
